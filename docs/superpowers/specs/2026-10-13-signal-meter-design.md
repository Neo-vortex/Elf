# Elf Signal Meter: 8-LED S-Meter with Overload Warning (Design Spec)

- **Date:** 2026-10-13
- **Status:** Design approved in conversation (user, 2026-10-13); this document awaits the user's review.
- **Extends:** phase 3 spec `2026-10-12-phase3-board-circuits-design.md` §3.3 (controller hardware) and phase 4 spec `2026-10-13-phase4-firmware-virtual-board-design.md` (firmware, virtual board).
- **Delivered as:** plan 4.3, after plan 4.2 (the virtual board).

## 1. Goal and decisions

An 8-LED bar on the board shows the strength of the signal being listened to, as an S-meter, and warns when the ADC approaches clipping.

Decisions (user, 2026-10-13):
1. **What it shows:** S-meter of the tuned slice by default; the top LEDs blink as an overload warning when the ADC nears full scale (option "both").
2. **Scale:** classic S-meter steps, S9 = −73 dBm at the antenna, 6 dB per S-unit; the LEDs light at S1, S3, S5, S7, S9, S9+10, S9+20, S9+40.
3. **Drive:** a fourth 74HC595 at the end of the relay chain with its own latch pin.

Assumed (open to correction): colours 5 green (S1–S9), 2 yellow (S9+10, S9+20), 1 red (S9+40 and overload).

**Success criteria:**
- A station at a known level at the antenna lights the LEDs its S-reading predicts, in the virtual board fed by the twin (e.g. −95 dBm → S5 → 3 LEDs; −73 dBm → S9 → 5 LEDs).
- A near-full-scale input blinks LED7 and LED8.
- Meter updates never re-latch the relays.
- `SM0;` returns the same reading over CAT, so Hamlib-based programs show it.

## 2. Hardware (controller block)

- **U_LED:** 74HC595 on 3V3_D (100 nF decoupling). SER ← the third relay register's QH'; SRCLK shared with the relay chain; RCLK = `led_latch`, a new GPIO; /OE shared with the relay registers (pulled up, so dark until the firmware enables outputs); /SRCLR to 3V3_D; its QH' tied off through 10 kΩ (the old chain end tie moves here).
- **LEDs:** 8 × 0805, each from Qk through 1 kΩ to the LED anode, cathode to GND: ≈ 1.4 mA per LED at 3.3 V (green Vf ≈ 2.0 V), ≤ 12 mA with all lit, inside the 74HC595's 70 mA package limit and the 3V3_D budget (spec §3.2 amendment: 0.15 A continuous). LED k = Q(k−1): LED1 = QA (S1) … LED8 = QH (S9+40, red).
- **GPIO:** `led_latch` on a free pad that is not PSRAM (35–37), strapping (0, 3, 45, 46) or USB (19, 20); the controller check's GPIO rules apply unchanged.
- **Board check:** the rail table gains nothing new (74HC595 VCC on 3V3_D already); a test checks that LED k's anode resistor connects to U_LED Q(k−1) and that RCLK of U_LED is not the relay latch.
- **Layout note (layout phase):** the bar sits on the digital edge of the board with a silkscreen block "SIGNAL" and the S-marks under the LEDs.

## 3. Firmware: `core/meter.{c,h}`

- **Input:** the I/Q frames `audio_pump` moves into the ring (a tap, so the USB stream is untouched).
- **Slice:** complex mix by −offset (NCO, 32-bit phase accumulator), decimation by 16 (CIC, order 3) to 6 kHz, then a short FIR to a 2.5 kHz bandwidth; power = mean |x|² per 10 ms block.
- **Offset:** default +10 kHz (the convention of the project's tools: LO 10 kHz below the station); set by CAT `ZO<±offset Hz>;` (|offset| ≤ 43 kHz), read by `ZO;`.
- **Level:** dBm at the antenna = slice power in dBFS + calibration(filter, preamp, attenuator) in dB. The calibration table is generated by `firmware/tools/gen_tables.py` from the twin's hardware model (`neosim.hardware`: front-end gain at the filter's centre, conversion gain, baseband, ADC full scale), so firmware and twin share one source. A later calibration plan may trim it per board with the cal tone.
- **Ballistics:** attack within one 10 ms block; decay 300 ms time constant.
- **Display:** step k lights when level ≥ threshold k: −121 (S1), −109 (S3), −97 (S5), −85 (S7), −73 (S9), −63 (S9+10), −53 (S9+20), −33 (S9+40) dBm; a bar (all steps below the level lit).
- **Overload:** peak |I| or |Q| ≥ −3 dBFS within the last 50 ms → LED7 and LED8 blink at 4 Hz, held at least 1 s after the last peak; the bar's other LEDs keep showing the S-reading.
- **Output:** the 8 bits are shifted out with the relay bits (32 bits, bit 31 first) and only `led_latch` is pulsed, every 50 ms; no meter latch while a relay change is settling.
- **CAT:** `SM0;` → `SM0nnnn;` with nnnn = 0000–0030 (TS-2000 scale: S0–S9 = 0–15 in steps of ≈ 1.67, S9+60 = 30); `ZO` as above.

## 4. Virtual board and tests

- **vhw:** the chain grows to 32 bits; a separate latch model for U_LED; `vhw_led_outputs()`.
- **Twin link:** the state message is unchanged (LEDs are an output of the firmware, not an input to the twin).
- **C unit tests (`test_meter.c`):** tones at known dBFS through a known calibration give the expected step; decay time; overload blink and hold; offset moves the slice (a tone outside the slice does not light the bar); `SM0;` scale; relay bits unchanged by meter updates (the relay latch is never pulsed by the meter).
- **End-to-end (user-space USB/IP):** each five-band CW station (−95 dBm) reads S5 over `SM0;` (0008–0009) while its key is down; a −73 dBm carrier station reads S9 (0015 ± 1).
- **Board test:** LED k on U_LED Q(k−1), U_LED's RCLK separate from the relay latch.

## 5. Out of scope

Per-board absolute calibration of the meter (later calibration plan), PWM brightness, other display modes.
